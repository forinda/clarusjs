First release: `npx @clarusjs/ai mcp`, an MCP server for agents writing clarusjs. Tools: `check` (type errors and lint from the project's own `clarusjs check`), `explain` (where a built route's parts run), `compile` (one component: island or not, its output, warnings and lint) and `docs_search` (the clarusjs.com docs, cached for a day).
