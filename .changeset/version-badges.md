Version badges in Markdown: `{since=0.16.0}`, `{deprecated=0.17.0}` and `{removed=0.18.0}` (combinable: `{deprecated=0.17.0 removed=0.18.0}`) after a heading, in a table cell or in text render as `<span class="version" data-version="since">Since 0.16.0</span>`, left out of the heading's id, anchor label and the page's contents. The docs theme styles them.
