Chip input: a field where each value typed becomes a chip (tags, email addresses). Enter or a comma adds, Backspace removes the last, × removes one; `[(values)]`, hidden inputs with `[name]`, and a cancelable `(change)`. `ui.css` styles it, and its chip and input rules are now a `chips` section shared with the combobox.
