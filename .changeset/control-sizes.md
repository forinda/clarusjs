One size scale for controls: `--ui-control-sm`, `--ui-control-md` and `--ui-control-lg` (30, 36 and 44px at the default `--ui-space`, so Density scales them). Fields, selects, comboboxes and toggles take their height from it, and `data-size="sm"` or `"lg"` on a control or anything around it picks the size.
