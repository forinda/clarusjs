The Learn path beside a lesson (from 1024px): the course and how many lessons are done, each lesson with the XP it can earn and its `minutes` (frontmatter), marked done, next or locked from the progress in this browser, the steps of the lesson you're on following the lesson bar, and your XP and badges linking to the progress page. Lesson items get `xp` (counted from the page: 50, plus 10 a step and 20 a quiz) and `minutes`. New `--xp` and `--xp-soft` tokens color XP and badges in both themes.
