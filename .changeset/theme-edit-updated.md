"Last updated" and edit links from a pattern: `themeDocs()` pages carry their source `file` and last commit time (`updated`, one `git log`), and with `config.pages` the layout shows the date and builds `editLink: '…/edit/main/src/pages/:file'`. Dynamic routes are listed (hidden from the sidebar) so their edit links resolve.
