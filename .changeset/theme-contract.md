`LayoutInputs` (from `nav.ts`): the inputs a site's layout passes to a theme's `layout.tscl`, the contract a theme of your own implements, kept equal to the docs theme's layout by a test. The new "Writing a theme" guide lists what the package makes public (covered by semver) and what is internal, with a minimal theme that is built and type-checked in CI.
