Quizzes: `quiz.tscl` (question, an island for the options with Check and a polite announcement, the answer and explanation in a `<details>`). In a lesson the action bar's button becomes Check and turns green or red; right and wrong are also marked in words. Green and red tokens (`--ok`, `--bad`) are part of the theme.
