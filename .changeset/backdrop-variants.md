Dialog backdrop variants: `data-backdrop="light" | "dark" | "blur" | "none"` on a dialog (and so on sheets and alert dialogs), read through two knobs, `--ui-backdrop-opacity` (0.45) and `--ui-backdrop-blur` (0px), which you can also set on `:root` for a site-wide default.
